## Critical and High triage

1. `BE-GAP-001` is the only Critical candidate: selected-source continuity is lost at the public reject/regenerate wiring boundary, creating a wrong-database authorization/execution risk.
2. `BE-GAP-002` through `BE-GAP-009` are High: masked data persistence, deletion cancellation, configured timeout, retry quota, retry audit, health/readiness, OpenAPI contract, and migration rollback.
3. Remaining candidates are bounded Mid/Low operational, concurrency, data-invariant, external-provider, exposure, and configuration gaps.
